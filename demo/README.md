# Demo kit (Owner: Henoch)

Put these here as they get written:

- `script.md`: the 90-second judge demo, word for word, with who does what.
- `pitch.md`: the 3-minute pitch outline ("see the shot before you practise it").
- `backup-video.md`: where the recorded backup demo lives and how to play it offline.
- `rehearsal-log.md`: each rehearsal, its time, and what broke.

Rule: anything simulated or mocked in the demo is labelled as such on screen.
